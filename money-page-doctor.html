How to Clear Cache Without Losing Your Token

IMPORTANT: Your GitHub token is stored in IndexedDB. Follow these steps to keep it safe when clearing cache!

Method 1: Chrome Clear Browsing Data (SAFEST)

  1. Press Cmd + Shift + Delete (Mac) or Ctrl + Shift + Delete (Windows)
  2. In the popup window:
    • CHECK: "Cached images and files"
    • UNCHECK: "Cookies and other site data"
  3. Click "Clear data"

Method 2: Site-Specific Settings

  1. Click the lock icon in the address bar (left of the URL)
  2. Click "Cookies and site data"
  3. Click "Manage on-device site data"
  4. You'll see aboutwallart.github.io listed
  5. DO NOT click the trash icon next to it!
  6. Just click "Done" to keep your token safe

Where is Your Token Stored?

Your GitHub token is saved in IndexedDB (browser database) for the site aboutwallart.github.io. As long as you don't delete "Cookies and site data" for this specific site, your token will survive cache clears!

What Deletes Your Token?

  • Clearing "Cookies and other site data" for ALL sites
  • Deleting site data specifically for aboutwallart.github.io
  • Clicking the trash icon next to aboutwallart.github.io in site settings
  • Using "Clear all data" or "Reset browser" options
Pro Tip: Always keep a backup of your token in a secure text file on your computer, just in case!
