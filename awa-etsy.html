Apply to a duplicate

Etsy can't set international prices by API, so you duplicate a listing on Etsy (the copy keeps those prices) and this copies everything else onto it. The duplicate's SKU must end in "B"; its original is the same SKU without the "B". It copies the title, description, tags, materials, section, attributes and the Shopify photos — and never touches prices or variations.

⚠️ This edits a LIVE Etsy draft. Check the match before you apply.

Clean listings (remove off-platform text)

Etsy removes listings that point buyers off-platform. This strips everything below the "FREE UK POSTAGE" line (social links, bit.ly, email, the ★★ promos) from your existing listings. Scan first to preview — nothing is changed until you click Apply, and every original description is backed up to GitHub.

⚠️ This edits your LIVE Etsy listings. Always Scan and review the preview before Apply.
