Video Content Plan

Goal: turn social video into store sales. Built around your 37 styled-in-room videos.

How to read this: Sections 1–8 are the rules (set once). Section 9 is your full post-by-post calendar — all 37 videos, ready to schedule. Section 10 is the Outfy image fix.

1. The strategy in one line

Post one styled-in-room video a day as a Reel across Instagram, TikTok and Facebook, push the same clip to Twitter/X with a direct product link, and send every viewer to a clear place to buy. Video opens the door to new people; the link (and later the in-app shops) closes the sale.
Why video, and why now: You already post images 3–4×/day and reach has flatlined — so volume isn't the problem. The platforms now push video to new people. On Facebook your Reels already pull a third of all views with barely any effort; even your abandoned TikToks hit 250–700 views with 11 followers. Video is the lever that actually moves new buyers.

3. Video format & size (the master file)

Make one master video per clip in this format — it works everywhere:

  • Shape: Vertical 9:16
  • Size: 1080 × 1920 px
  • File: MP4, H.264
  • Length: 15–30 seconds (under 60s max). Shorter usually wins.
  • First second: show the finished room + art immediately. No slow intro.

Optional: a 4:5 (1080×1350) crop for the feed — but the 9:16 Reel is the priority.

Text safe zone (important): On every platform, buttons and captions cover the edges. Keep all on-screen text in the middle 60% of the screen — avoid the top ~250px (profile/clock) and the bottom ~500px (caption, like/share buttons, progress bar). If text sits too low, TikTok and IG hide it.

4. On-screen text rules

  • One idea per video. One hook, one message.
  • Hook appears in the first second — a question or promise (see formulas below).
  • Big, clean white text with a soft shadow so it reads on any wall colour. Matches your minimalist brand.
  • End card on the last 2–3 seconds: the product name + a soft call to action (e.g. "Shop the look 🔗").
  • Never say "real customer" or imply a testimonial. Frame as styled in a real room.

Hook formulas (rotate these)

  • "POV: your [bedroom] finally feels finished."
  • "3 prints. One blank wall. Watch the difference."
  • "The wall art everyone asks me about 👇"
  • "How to style art above your [bed / sofa / crib]."
  • "Nursery wall ideas that aren't boring."
  • "What is wall art actually for? (it's not just decoration)"

5. Music

  • Instagram & TikTok: use a trending sound from inside the app (tap the music note → "trending"). Trending audio = more reach. Keep it calm/aesthetic to match the brand.
  • Facebook & YouTube: use the app's built-in licensed music (calm/lo-fi/acoustic). Don't rip TikTok audio onto these — it can get muted.
  • Vibe: soft, warm, "cosy home" feeling. Never loud or busy.

6. Caption formula (same idea, tweak per platform)

Structure: Hook line → one benefit → soft CTA → where to buy → hashtags.

Example (living room set):
That empty wall above the sofa? Sorted. 🛋️
Our 3-piece framed set instantly warms up a living room — no drilling skills required.
👉 Tap the link in bio to shop this exact set.
#wallart #livingroomdecor #homedecor #gallerywall #interiordesign
  • Instagram: "link in bio" + product name. 5–10 hashtags.
  • TikTok: shorter, casual. 3–5 hashtags. "link in bio".
  • Facebook: 1–2 hashtags only. Can paste the product link directly in the caption.
  • Twitter/X: short line + direct product link in the tweet (this is the whole point — it's your best sales path).

8. Posting times (UK)

Your audience is busiest mornings and midday, with a real overnight spike (US/international followers). Best windows:

  • Main slot — 8:00–9:00 am UK (your strongest daily window)
  • Backup — 12:00–1:00 pm UK
  • Evening reuse — 7:00–9:00 pm UK (catches US daytime)

Post the Reel once in the morning slot; if you cross-post to Facebook/Twitter later the same day, use the evening slot.

9. The post-by-post calendar (37 videos)

How to use these cards: one video per day. Each card has everything you need, colour-coded so you can find it fast: purple = Kling prompt green = on-screen text (pick one) blue = captions. Every block has its own Copy button.
Scheduling in Metricool (recommended): you can schedule the whole month and tag your products right inside Metricool for Instagram & Facebook (catalogue), so the video links straight to the product — no manual links. One setup catch: connect your Instagram to Metricool through Facebook (not directly as Instagram), and make sure the products are approved in your IG Shop — otherwise the “Tag product” option won’t appear. On TikTok, schedule the video in Metricool, then add the TikTok Shop product tag in-app. On Twitter/X the full product link is already in the tweet text, so it just posts and works.
Captions: red ❤️ throughout, written to paste and go. Instagram links aren’t clickable in captions (that’s an IG rule) — so the product is reached by the product tag on the Reel, not a “link in bio”. Facebook & X carry the full clickable URL.
Build order: Days 1–18 = new videos (generate each in Kling using the purple prompt) · Days 19–37 = videos you’ve already filmed (just caption & schedule). Reorder freely in Metricool — the day numbers are a running order, not fixed dates.
